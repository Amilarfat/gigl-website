About the school

A small school with large intentions

GIGL International School was started with one belief — that a child in Darbhanga deserves the same quality of teaching, technology and care as a child in any metro city. Not someday. From the very first day of Nursery.

So we built small on purpose: small classes where every child is known by name, digital smart classrooms in every room, teachers who explain patiently instead of rushing the syllabus, and two engineers on the board who keep the technology actually working — not just advertised. Parents are partners here, not visitors; meetings are regular, feedback is written, and the gate is always open for a respectful conversation.

Our Vision

To be Darbhanga's most trusted school for early and primary education.

Our Mission

To teach with clarity, warmth and honesty — and to make learning joyful every day.

GIGL International School

Welcome & philosophy

GIGL International School is a English-medium school in Darbhanga, Bihar, offering the CBSE pattern curriculum from Nursery to Class 6th. Our classes are small, our classrooms are digital, and our teachers know every child by name.

  • Activity based learning — concepts are seen and touched, not memorised.
  • Digital classrooms with projector and audio in every room.
  • A safe, nurturing campus designed at a child's height.
  • Parents are partners — regular meetings, open communication.

Leadership

Mohammad Afzal Sir (B.Ed) — Principal & Director, leads academic vision and pedagogy.

Er. Hasan Sir — Director, leads STEM innovation, digital infrastructure and coding.

Er. Iftekhar Sir — Director, leads student welfare, sports, safety and operations.

Academics

  • Early Years (Nursery–UKG): phonics, pre-writing, number sense, story, art, free play.
  • Foundation Years (Class 1–4): English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS, Computer, Art, Music, PE.
  • Growing Years (Class 5–6): English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Computer.

Assessment is continuous and descriptive — no public ranking of small children.

STREAM & clubs

Science, Technology, Recreation, Engineering, Art and Mathematics are woven into the weekly timetable through labs, projects and club time.

  • Ten clubs: English Literary, Hindi Sahitya, Young Scientist, Photography, Orator's Public Speaking, Creative Expression, Eco Club, Music, Dance, Sports.
  • Coding from Class 3 in the computer and robotics lab.

Facilities

  • Digital smart classrooms
  • Computer & robotics lab
  • English language lab
  • Library with graded readers
  • Sports arena and morning drill
  • GPS tracked van transport
  • Infirmary and counselling room

Admission guidelines

  • Nursery 3+, LKG 4+, UKG 5+, Class 1 5½–6 years as on 31 March.
  • Submit the online enquiry, then visit the campus with documents.
  • Documents: birth certificate, 4 photographs, Aadhaar, previous report card, address proof.
  • Fees and transport routes are shared at the campus office.
