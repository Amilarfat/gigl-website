Principal's welcome

A message from Mohammad Afzal Sir, Principal & Director

Dear parents,

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions you will make for your child. At GIGL International School we keep three promises: a teacher who knows your child by name, a classroom that works, and a campus where your child is safe and happy.

“Every child who walks through our gate deserves a teacher who believes in them before the world does.”

We are a small school on purpose. Small classes mean individual attention, honest feedback and a teacher who notices when a child is quiet. Our classrooms are digital, our playground is open, and our doors are open to parents every day.

I invite you to visit the campus, sit in a class, and speak to our teachers. That is the best way to know if GIGL is the right home for your child.

Mohammad Afzal Sir (B.Ed)
Principal & Director, GIGL International School

Curriculum model

CBSE pattern, adapted for young learners

The GIGL curriculum follows the CBSE pattern and is organised into three stages, each matched to the child's age and development.

  • Early Years (Nursery–UKG): play, phonics, number sense, art, rhyme, social skills.
  • Foundation Years (Class 1–4): English, Hindi, Mathematics, EVS, Computer, Art, Music, PE.
  • Growing Years (Class 5–6): English, Hindi, Sanskrit, Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Computer.

Assessment is continuous and descriptive. We do not publish class rankings for small children.

Teaching & learning

How a GIGL class actually runs

  • Activity based learning — concepts are seen, touched and understood, not memorised.
  • Digital smart classes with projector and audio in every room.
  • Inquiry-based approaches that let children ask questions and find answers.
  • Individual attention — teachers restructure knowledge to match each child's pace.
  • Home room periods for classroom discussion on values and life skills.
  • Regular handwriting, reading and language enrichment sessions.
“Technology in a classroom should be invisible — the learning should be the only thing a child notices.” — Er. Hasan Sir, Director

Early Years programme

Nursery · LKG · UKG (age 3 to 5)

The kindergarten inculcates curiosity, exploration and discovery in a fun-filled, safe and happy learning environment. An individualised approach nurtures a love of learning, social skills and moral values.

  • Phonics, pre-writing and number sense
  • Story, rhyme, art, music and free play
  • Toilet training support and a rest routine
  • No formal exams — observation and gentle guidance only
  • English communication from the very first day

Foundation years programme

Co-scholastic programme

Learning that happens outside the classroom

Several skills and qualities that are instrumental to a child's holistic development are learnt outside the classroom. GIGL has a mandatory co-curricular programme that fully supports a child's learning process.

  • Performing arts: art, craft, music, dance, theatre, short film making.
  • Sports: outdoor games, morning drill, inter-house competitions.
  • Excursions: educational trips that let children learn by experiencing.
  • Academia-industry interface: interaction with professionals and entrepreneurs.
  • Child counselling: wellbeing support for children and parents.

A word to our parents

Cooperation makes the school work

In order to secure all that is best for the education of your ward, the maximum cooperation between parents and school authorities is necessary. We earnestly recommend that parents look into their child's school diary every day and see to it that the lessons and homework assigned for the next day are done.

Children should be encouraged to cultivate cleanliness habitually. Remarks made in the diary should be read and acknowledged. Please attend parent-teacher meetings and stay in touch with the class teacher.
